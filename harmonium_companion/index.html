pianoPlay notes

Click an on-screen key, play with a MIDI keyboard, or use these computer keys:

Lower rangeA W S E D F T G Y H U J
Upper rangeK O L P ; '

Keys follow chromatic order: white-note keys alternate with the in-between notes. Click an on-screen key or play with a MIDI keyboard as well.

Right-click and drag the on-screen keyboard to scroll across its full range.

music_noteRaga & pitch

Choose a raga
Use the left and right arrows beside its name.
Filter notes
Click the raga name to restrict play to its notes; click again to turn filtering off.
Change notation
Switch between Western note names and Indian Sargam.
Change octave
Select Low, Middle, or High to move the keyboard range.
Transpose
Use − or + to shift pitch by semitones.

tuneShape your sound

Manual bellows
Turn off Auto bellows, then press Spacebar to pump air while playing.
Hold notes
Hold Shift while playing, or turn on Hold notes to sustain notes.
Sub-octave & Coupler
Add a lower or upper octave to the notes you play.
Sound settings
Adjust Volume, Sustain, Chorus, and Reverb to taste.
MIDI input
Select a connected MIDI device from MIDI input.
